The AI Data Readiness Survey

Operators keep telling us the same thing: AI pilots stall on data, not models. Fragmented systems, inconsistent definitions, and quality problems decide what AI can actually do long before anyone picks a tool. This survey asks how ready your data really is, and what stalls when it is not. Twelve questions, about three minutes. Findings will be published anonymously on the ZAI Insights page.
