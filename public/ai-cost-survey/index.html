The AI Cost of Compute Survey

AI spend rarely behaves like other line items. Consumption pricing, surprise bills, and costs buried in other budgets make it hard to see, forecast, and control. This survey asks what AI actually costs your organization and how you keep it in check. Twelve questions, about three minutes. Findings will be published anonymously on the ZAI Insights page.
