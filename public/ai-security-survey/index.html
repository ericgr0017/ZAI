The AI Security & Compliance Survey

Executives keep telling us the same two stories: staff quietly use AI tools nobody approved, and clients now ask hard questions about the AI tools that are approved. This survey asks what safeguards are really in place, where data actually goes, and who answers for it. Twelve questions, about three minutes. Findings will be published anonymously on the ZAI Insights page.
